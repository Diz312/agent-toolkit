## Proposed

- **[session-report](https://github.com/anthropics/claude-plugins-official/tree/main/plugins/session-report)** (plugin, claude-code, context-engineering, score 80): Official. Builds an HTML report from `~/.claude/projects` transcripts: tokens, cache hit rate, subagents, skills and the most expensive prompts. The analyzer only reads local files (no network, no child processes); the template loads Google Fonts. Audit clean. Needs `node` on PATH.
